# Pikki's Info Roadmap

Feature ideas to make Pikki's Info the go-to place for wardrobe and crafting, alongside Nikki Calc's stage scoring. Nothing here is built yet.

## Quick wins

- **Safe-to-decompose list** — a personal list of extra items whose crafts and evolutions are done, with how many copies can safely go (Nikki's Info's signature feature).
- **Wardrobe file import** — import the game's `clothes_date` file, Nikki's Info `.wbak` backups, or Nikki Calc wardrobes, then sync to your account.
- **Suit completion tracker** — progress for every suit (posed and unposed), mark whole suits owned, and optionally show it on your public profile.

## Next

- **Evolution and recolour planner** — shows how many base copies you still need for the evolutions and dyes you want.
- **What's new that you don't own** — new items filtered to ones you don't have, want, or need for a crafting tree.
- **Links to Nikki Calc for stage scores** — show which stages an item tops and link to Nikki Calc, complementing it rather than competing.

## Bigger projects

- **Recipe and gold shopping list** — turn a wanted item or suit into the copies, gold, and dyes still needed.
- **Attribute and colour search** — find items by attributes, tags, colour, and rarity (a favourite Nikki's Info feature).
- **Where-to-get-it info** — drops, stores, and events for missing items; needs a second data source (e.g. the Love Nikki wiki, loadoutgg, or community sheets) because Nikki Calc doesn't include obtain locations.
